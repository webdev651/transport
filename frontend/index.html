System Features

A complete transportation management solution for students, drivers and administrators.

Student Information Management

Keep each student's school details, status and pass validity in one registry.

QR Code Generation

Every approved student gets a scannable digital QR pass they can download.

Secure QR Verification

Each scan is checked against the student's current status, with an optional face check at boarding.

Student Registry Management

Add, approve, flag, revoke or reinstate student passes from one place.

Transportation Discount Verification

Eligible students get the discounted fare applied automatically when their pass is scanned.

Ride and Scan History

Students review their own rides, and administrators see every scan in order.

Driver Management

Drivers sign in to a dedicated area and manage their own account details.

Reports and Monitoring

Follow weekly verification volume and the operator subsidy ledger at a glance.

Notifications and Announcements

Students are notified when their pass is approved, updated, flagged or revoked.

One pass for every student ride

TransitPass replaces paper IDs and manual checks with a single QR pass. Students carry one digital pass, drivers confirm eligibility with a scan, and administrators keep an accurate registry and a complete record of every verification.

Students

Register once, keep the QR pass on a phone and check ride history any time.

Drivers

Scan a pass at boarding and see right away whether the discount applies.

Administrators

Approve students, manage passes and review scan history and reports.
